# Vendored: tstack

The skills in this folder (`babysit-pr`, `council`, `file-pr`, `full-send`,
`full-stop`, `leaving-pr-comments`, `modernize`, `sol-loop`, `takeover`) are
copied verbatim from [t3dotgg/tstack](https://github.com/t3dotgg/tstack) at
commit `1085050f3baed1ac724caa4a8166145fb2ac88af`, under the MIT license in
`TSTACK-LICENSE`.

To update, copy `skills/*` from a newer checkout over these folders and bump
the commit above. Put personal tweaks (council members, etc.) in `CLAUDE.md`
rather than editing the skills, so updates stay a straight copy.
